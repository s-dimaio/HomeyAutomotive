Privacy Policy

Homey for Android Automotive

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in the Homey for Android Automotive application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience while controlling your smart home from your vehicle.

2. Information Collection

We believe in data minimization. The App is designed to operate without transmitting any of your personal identification data to developer-owned servers.

  • Personal Identification Data: We do not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Location Data (Precise & Background Location): To support automated smart home geofencing features, the App accesses your vehicle's precise and approximate location data (GPS coordinates). When you enable the "Smart Alerts" feature, the App accesses this location data in the background (even when the App is closed or not in use).
  • Authentication: All authentication is handled through the official Athom B.V. (Homey) OAuth2 system. The App never sees or stores your Homey password.
  • Local Storage: To function, the App securely stores a temporary access token and your Homey hub's approximate home coordinates on your vehicle's encrypted local storage. This data is used exclusively to communicate with the Homey Web API and manage local geofences, and can be cleared at any time.

3. Location Data Access, Usage & Background Collection

The App requests access to location data (including ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) strictly to enable safety and convenience geofencing automations for your vehicle:

  • Departure Security Warnings: When your vehicle exits your home geofence perimeter, the App verifies the status of your doors, gates, and garage doors. If an access point was left open, it displays an in-car heads-up notification so you can secure your home.
  • Arrival Access Prompts: When your vehicle approaches your home geofence perimeter, the App displays a timely in-car notification with a one-tap shortcut allowing you to open your gate or garage door before pulling into the driveway.
  • Homey Companion App & Automations: Geofence transition events (entering or exiting your home perimeter) communicate directly with your personal Homey Pro hub to query device states, trigger user-configured Homey Flows (such as arrival or departure routines), and automate entry points. Your raw GPS coordinates and driving routes are processed strictly on your vehicle and are never transmitted to your Homey hub or any external servers.
  • Background Location Usage: To reliably detect entry and exit events while you are driving, the vehicle's location must be monitored even when the App is closed or not actively open on the infotainment display (e.g., while navigation, media, or other car applications are running in the foreground).
  • Local Processing & No External Tracking: All geofence boundary calculations occur locally on your vehicle via Android operating system location services. Location data is never uploaded to developer servers, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising, marketing, analytics, or behavioral tracking.
  • User Choice & Control: Location access is completely optional. You can grant, deny, or revoke location permissions at any time through the vehicle's system settings (Settings → Apps → Homey → Permissions → Location) or directly in the App's settings screen. If location permissions are declined, the App remains fully functional for all manual device controls and dashboard monitoring.

4. Information Usage

Any information accessed by the App is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To display the status of your smart home devices (e.g., lights, locks, sensors) on your car's dashboard.
  • To allow you to execute Homey Flows and control devices directly from the infotainment system.
  • To trigger smart geofencing routines and alerts (departure safety checks, arrival gate/garage prompts, and in-home arrival/departure flow automations) as detailed in Section 3.

5. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the Homey Web API provided by Athom B.V. When you use the App, your device data is processed by Athom according to their own privacy standards. We encourage you to review the official Homey Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Access tokens are stored locally on your device using standard Android security features to prevent unauthorized access. Since this is a server-less application, no data is stored or managed on any external servers controlled by the developer.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
